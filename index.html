About Me

Nice to meet you! I'm Eric Vaughn, a C/C++ programmer learning WebDev!

I earned my Associates of Computer Science from St. Charles Community College (SCC) in May of 2026. It was a great school with even better teachers. Definitely a great choice if you decide to go back to school — I highly recommend!

I'm a published poet in the 25th annual Mid Rivers Review! I hope to have more publications soon.

Many of my previous jobs have been in the service industry. I was a Customer Service & Pre-Sales Rep. at CarShield and a delivery driver for DoorDash. I am ready to get out of sales and delivery driving and with my Associates under my belt, I'm ready for a career in programming!

Like most people that have an interest in programming, I contracted the techy-bug through video games. Shocker, I know. Even though I don't have much time to play them anymore, I'm glad they lead me to my interest in programming. My life, quite literally, wouldn't be the same without them! I've met many friends and started a career path because of them. Who ever said you can't learn anything from video games?

There's a special place in my heart for all things artistic. Writing, painting, music, glass blowing, wood/stone carving. You name it, I like it!

By the way, I wrote every line of code to make this webpage possible. Pretty cool right?

Let's strive to get better everyday!
